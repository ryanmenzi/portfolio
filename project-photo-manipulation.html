Credits
Photography, collage construction, and digital compositing by Ryan Menzildjian. The piece was informed by artists like Daniel Gordon and Martin Parr, with additional source photos gathered through my own travel documentation.
Info
This fantasy first-person montage is about giving viewers a break from everyday weight and inviting them into a dreamlike world for a moment. I wanted it to feel magical, personal, and open-ended, with no extra characters, so each viewer can imagine their own story while moving through the scene.
