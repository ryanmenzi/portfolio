Build your own brand


Description:
The Final Project requires students to develop a complete brand from scratch,
applying the full methodology explored throughout the course.

Unlike previous assignments, this project is fully self-initiated:
students are responsible for defining the concept, identifying a clear audience,
and building a strong strategic foundation.
The project must demonstrate the ability to move from research to insight,
from strategy to narrative, and from narrative to a coherent visual system.

Students are expected to make intentional decisions, define a clear point of view,
and translate it into a consistent and flexible brand experience.

The final outcome will be presented as a structured pitch showing clarity,
coherence, and the ability to justify both strategic and visual choices.
