Concept, rules design, visual identity, and production mockups by Ryan Menzildjian. The game takes inspiration from Codenames and Pictionary and turns it into a design-thinking activity with cards, rule pamphlets, and physical components.
Info
Brief Cipher is a fast team game where a Lead Designer gets 60 seconds to sketch selected process cards from a shared 5x5 grid. Teammates guess the clue, score by difficulty, and learn design language through play. After critique, I shifted the system from concept cards to process-focused cards and refined typography, color, and layout for quick readability.
